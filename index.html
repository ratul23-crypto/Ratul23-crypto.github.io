Is Student Tools BD free?

Yes. The calculators and study tools available on this website are designed to be free to use.

Yes. The website is responsive and designed to work on smartphones, tablets and computers.

Will more tools be added?

Yes. More student-focused calculators and educational resources can be added in future updates.
